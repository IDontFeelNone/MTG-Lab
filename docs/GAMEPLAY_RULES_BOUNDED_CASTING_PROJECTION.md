## Four selected casting stages

### 601.2b: choices and cost declarations

The record describes mode choice, splice declarations with selected cards
revealed from hand, alternative and additional cost declarations, variable
values, hybrid payment declarations, Phyrexian payment declarations and
restrictions imposed by prior choices.
If a variable depends on a choice ordinarily made later, that choice is
brought into the declaration stage descriptively. The record preserves the
restriction against simply combining multiple alternative methods or
alternative costs for one spell. None of these choices is executed.

Mode semantics depend on unprojected `700.2`; splice on `702.47`;
alternative/additional costs on `118.8` and `118.9`; variable values on
`107.3`. The selected `601.2f` provides the later total-cost-stage relationship.
Hybrid and Phyrexian branches remain descriptive and unresolved for execution.
No Card text, actual spell, modes, X value or payment selection is interpreted.

### 601.2f: total cost and lock-in

The base is the normal mana cost **OR** a selected alternative cost. Additional
costs and increases are represented before reductions. When multiple
reductions apply, their order is chosen by the player; the projection imposes
no fixed sequence. The mana portion's floor at zero is represented. Direct
total-cost effects follow those ordinary components, and the resulting total
becomes locked;
later changes do not silently replace that locked total. The rule explicitly
links to selected `601.2b`.

The Madness invariant survives source verification: a Madness cost is a
candidate **alternative base cost**, never a discount from normal mana cost.
These records neither compute costs nor apply effects, reductions, layers or
runtime lock transitions.

### 601.2g: conditional mana-ability opportunity

When the determined total includes mana payment, the record describes an
opportunity to use mana abilities before costs are paid. Direct dependency
`605` remains **UNRESOLVED / UNPROJECTED**. There is no mana generation,
mana-pool processing, activation, priority handling or payment implementation.
Fictional mana behavior supplies no authority for real `605` semantics.

### 601.2h: payment groups and prohibitions

The first group contains ordinary cost components and permits their payment
in any order. Components involving randomness or movement from a library to
a public zone belong to the later group, also with order choices inside that
group. Partial payment is prohibited, and an unpayable cost cannot be paid.
The projection captures those conditions without inspecting a library,
randomizing, sacrificing, discarding, tapping, paying life or moving objects.
No payment result or legally cast spell is produced.
