## Target requirement: 115.6

The PM observation distinguishes `target_requirement_semantics` from both
`current_targets_chosen` and `target_legality`. Selecting zero targets does not,
by itself, establish that an ability has no target requirement. Both `605.1a`
and `605.1b` need the requirement distinction when testing their no-target
criterion. This is a classification dependency, not a target-selection result.

Unknown required semantics must remain INDETERMINATE or UNSUPPORTED.
