# Mystery Booster 2 Rule Research

**Status:** Phase 65 evidence sufficiency assessment complete

## Boundary

Phase 65 establishes the evidence-backed bridge between research and future
canonical rule population. Archived source bytes remain under `data/sources/`,
processed research artifacts remain under `data/intermediate/research/`, and
canonical Product, Slot, Print Sheet, Printing, and Card data remains separate.
The Rule Claim Matrix cannot approve or promote canonical data.

## Retained artifacts

The authoritative machine-readable research artifacts are:

```text
data/intermediate/research/mystery_booster_2/phase_65/rule-claim-matrix.json
data/intermediate/research/mystery_booster_2/phase_65/evidence-sufficiency-report.json
```

The matrix assigns stable identifiers to product-rule claims, classifies their
evidence status, cites canonical Source Records and content-verified evidence
artifacts where support exists, supplies precise locators, maps claims to Tier 0
entities, and retains uncertainty. Unsupported claims intentionally have no
supporting citations.

The report classifies every matrix claim exactly once as known, partially known,
or unknown. It separately records the facts blocking canonical rule population,
pack generation, and probability calculation, then assesses architectural
sufficiency without changing Tier 0.

## Evidence result

The retained evidence confirms Mystery Booster 2 product identity and supports
membership for four bounded Printing examples. It does not establish the
complete booster structure, Slots, complete pools, Print Sheet entries or
weights, replacement behavior, treatment selection, collation dependencies, or
probability distributions. The evidence archive therefore remains insufficient
for canonical rule population or pack generation.

Phase 65 archives the existing controlled official product-page title capture
as a content-verified evidence bundle. The capture is deliberately narrow and
adds no product-rule support beyond identity. Waves 1 and 2 remain the evidence
for the four partial membership examples.

## Architectural result

The Tier 0 relationship
`Product -> Slot -> Print Sheet -> Printing -> Card` is sufficient for all facts
supported by the current evidence: Product identity and partial Printing
membership can be represented without an extension. Sufficiency for the
complete Mystery Booster 2 product is indeterminate because no preserved
evidence describes actual Slot selection or collation behavior.

One architectural question is retained rather than resolved: if later evidence
establishes conditional, correlated, mutually exclusive, or sequenced selection
events, can the current single-sheet Slot contract represent them without
product-specific behavior? Phase 65 does not redesign Tier 0.
