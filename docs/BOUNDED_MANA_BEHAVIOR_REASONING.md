## Deterministic policy and inference limits

`behavior_policy.compute` accepts explicit proposed kind, the twelve criterion
inputs, kind-evidence references, exact retained rule facts, and an optional closed
priority hypothetical. It recomputes the existing classification and method
result. Card names, subject IDs, provider mana annotations, prose substrings, and
original proposed labels cannot enter its generic calculation. Card selection and
provenance belong to `behavior.py` and the existing pilot adapter.

A branch requires the appropriate kind, a completed applicable method, resolved
active criterion inputs, the corresponding actual classification, and exact
bound rule facts. A method failure or incomplete assessment is not a substantive
non-mana property. Conditional exclusion alone never implies stack placement.
Malformed or stale evidence rejects admission; valid unresolved semantics produce
explicit unresolved outcomes. Missing relevant facts establish no completed
behavior consequence; altered supplied facts reject rather than becoming unknown.

| Branch | Required inference and retained support | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional activated mana | Activated kind and completed eligibility; 605.3b supplies no stack entry and immediate resolution after activation; 605.3a supplies opportunity categories; 605.3c restricts another activation of the same ability until resolution finishes; 405.6c supplies immediate-resolution and conditional activated-priority context; 605.2 separates classification, usability, and output. | Present permission, paid costs, an actual activation, output, an executed sequence, or restrictions on targeting/countering the permanent or all actions by an opponent. |
| Conditional triggered mana | Triggered kind and completed eligibility; 605.4a supplies no-stack treatment and immediate-after-causing-mana-ability context without waiting for priority; 605.2 preserves the output distinction. | Trigger detection, actual causality or output, activated opportunity categories, or activated priority return. No literal 605.4a → 405.6c citation is invented. |
| Conditional ordinary activated | Activated kind, completed conditional exclusion on substantive negatives, and 602.2/602.2a supply a noncard ability object at the top of the stack in the ordinary activation proposal. | Legal activation, successful completion, actual priority, a response window, a legal response, or applicability of any counterspell. |
| Conditional spell proposal | Explicit spell kind, 605.5b exclusion, and 601.2a supply stack entry at proposal start. | Successful casting completion, casting during another spell's resolution, or using the spell to satisfy an outstanding payment. Spell facts do not flow into activated branches. |
| Unresolved ordinary triggered frontier | Triggered kind and completed exclusion identify the ordinary triggered route through 605.5a/605.4. | General trigger placement, ordering, priority, and response behavior, because 603 is absent. No stack-placement conclusion follows from exclusion alone. |
| Unresolved behavior | Incomplete, indeterminate, unsupported, conflicting active readings, or missing relevant facts preserve uncertainty. | Either a completed mana exception or an ordinary placement default. |

The 405.6c mana-plus-additional-effect explanation is conditional on both effects
belonging to the same qualifying ability. No subject is assigned an additional
effect, and no fine-grained ordering between effect parts is invented.

Every consequence binds the pending interpretation, computed classification and
method, rule/package/projection/anchor evidence, antecedents, conditional result,
remaining unknowns, and authority. The report supplies the exact candidate and
applicable successor identities through shared context. Historical provider
observations remain historical observations, not current Oracle verification.

## Bounded priority demonstration

Ordinary reports contain no evidence that any player held priority. The public
priority report separately labels an explicit hypothetical assumption: the
selected ability qualifies under its pending reading, was activated, and finished
resolving. It never records those assumptions as real Card or game evidence.

| Assumed prior-priority premise | 405.6c implication for eligible activated mana only |
|---|---|
| `true` | Under all stated assumptions, the player who held priority immediately before activation receives priority after resolution. |
| `false` | That antecedent is absent; this rule establishes no return conclusion. This is not a prohibition on receiving priority under another rule. |
| `unknown` | Applicability of the return implication remains unknown. |

No converse is inferred. Triggered, non-mana, spell, and unresolved-classification
branches receive no activated-mana priority implication. The hypothetical is not
an interpretation revision, game observation, arbitrary scenario editor, game-state
schema, or executable state transition.

reproduction. Existing ownership limits also bound structure depth, node count,
