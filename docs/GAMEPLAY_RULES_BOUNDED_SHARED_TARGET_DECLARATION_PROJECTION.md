## Bounded descriptive knowledge and its limits

The new projection checks c's complete source against PR #201's retained
observation before representing the declaration stage. Required targets receive
object/player recipients that meet their stated criteria. Requirements tied to
a selected mode or alternative/additional cost depend on that prior choice;
without the relevant choice, those conditional requirements do not participate.
Cost-dependent alternative targeting permissions are also conditional, rather
than a generally available expansion of the recipient set.

A variable recipient count is declared before the recipients are identified.
Spell-defined counting information can determine that number. Once determined,
the count remains fixed even if the information used to determine it changes.
Within one occurrence of the targeting word, a recipient cannot fill multiple
slots; across separate occurrences, reuse can occur once for each occurrence
when the corresponding criteria permit it. Mandatory targeting obligations
require the largest compatible set of choices, while all applicable
prohibitions still constrain the result. These findings describe constraints;
they do not calculate a legal set or solve compatibility.

Chosen recipients acquire targeted status at this stage, with corresponding
becoming-targeted trigger consequences then; placement on the stack is deferred
until casting is complete. Those consequences remain descriptive knowledge
even though trigger execution and casting completion are unsupported.

Casting provenance is retained. Activated abilities consume the bounded
casting-stage knowledge through the retained `602.2b` range import; the
procedures are not declared interchangeable. This evidence supplies no
universal activated-mana trigger or priority rule.

Three concepts stay separate: **requiring targets**, **actual chosen-target
count**, and **targeted status**. Retained `115.6` permits a target requirement
to persist when zero targets are chosen, while its targeted status requires
one or more actually chosen targets. Zero chosen targets therefore cannot
prove absence of a target requirement or establish mana-ability eligibility.
This projection supplies no parser, requirement recognizer, legal-target-set
generator, actual choice algorithm, compatibility solver or Card/effect
interpretation.
