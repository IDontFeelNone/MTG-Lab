# Mystery Booster 2 Pack Observations

This directory stores reported pack-opening observations for Mystery Booster 2.

Observations are non-canonical records of what an observer reports opening. They may support later research, but they do not establish product rules, complete card pools, slot definitions, print sheets, weights, replacement behavior, collation, or probabilities.

## Layout

```text
boxes/
  box_001/
    manifest.json
    pack_001.json
```

Each pack preserves the reported card order and any explicitly reported treatment or frame information. Unreported attributes remain unknown rather than inferred.

Observation records must remain separate from canonical data and evidence-based rule promotion.
