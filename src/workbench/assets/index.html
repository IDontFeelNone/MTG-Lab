How this works & privacy

All card labels and identities are your declarations. A label never resolves a canonical Card identity. Each local identity stays attached to its card when you duplicate a deck. Editing a shared card label changes its display label in both configurations.

Only the selected section is sampled, uniformly without replacement. Other sections are excluded. Cards seen is cumulative, not cards still in hand. Observation labels establish no turn, play/draw, mulligan or extra-draw rules.

A group declares interchangeable cards for this question. All group minima must be met jointly. Direct comparison requires the same member identities, minima, selected section and observation schedule.

Inputs and reports stay in this application’s memory. Nothing is automatically saved or exported. Reloading loses your work. Download a report explicitly if you want to keep it; downloaded files contain your labels and deck quantities.
